About Us · हाम्रो बारेमा

Trusted Drinking Water of Badikhel, Lalitpur

Danphe Pure Water (Danphe Suddha Jal · डाफें शुद्ध जल) is a local water company serving Badikhel, Lalitpur and nearby communities. Our water comes from the natural spring at Godawari muhan and is treated through specialized filtration and water technology to make it clean, safe and refreshing.
